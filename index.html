🎨 Theory & Belt Meanings

The Tenets of Taekwon-Do

Tenet Meaning
Courtesy Being polite, respectful and considerate.
Integrity Knowing right from wrong and having the conscience to act correctly.
Perseverance Continuing despite difficulties and setbacks.
Self-Control Controlling your emotions and actions.
Indomitable Spirit Having courage and determination that cannot easily be defeated.

Belt Colours

⚪ White Belt

Meaning: Innocence. The student has no previous knowledge of Taekwon-Do.

🟡 Yellow Belt

Meaning: Earth. The foundation from which the plant grows.

🟢 Green Belt

Meaning: Growth. The plant grows and develops.

🔵 Blue Belt

Meaning: Heaven. The plant grows towards the sky.

🔴 Red Belt

Meaning: Danger. The student develops control and warns opponents to stay away.

⚫ Black Belt

Meaning: Maturity and proficiency. It also represents the opposite of white.

Why Practise Patterns?

📜 Taekwon-Do History

What does Taekwon-Do mean? ＋

Tae — foot or kicking.

Kwon — fist or hand.

Do — way or art.

Taekwon-Do can therefore be understood as "The way of the foot and hand."

General Choi Hong Hi ＋

General Choi Hong Hi is widely recognised as the founder of Taekwon-Do and played a major role in its development and international spread.

The ITF ＋

The International Taekwon-Do Federation, known as the ITF, was founded in 1966.

The Development of Taekwon-Do ＋

Modern Taekwon-Do developed in Korea during the 20th century and was later introduced around the world.

The Purpose of Taekwon-Do ＋
  • Physical fitness
  • Confidence
  • Discipline
  • Respect
  • Self-control
  • Self-defence
  • Mental development
